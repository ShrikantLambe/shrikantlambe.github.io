TL;DR — The case in four lines
→A semantic layer translates raw data into business language — common names, shared metrics, governed definitions — so every tool and every team sees the same numbers.
→In 2026, three forces make it structurally necessary: AI analytics that can't be trusted without governed definitions, data products that create metric drift, and self-service pressure that BI tools alone can't solve.
→There are three deployment patterns — BI-native, platform-native, and universal — each with a different trade-off profile. Match the tool to your architecture today.
→The real work is organizational: getting teams to agree on definitions. The technical implementation follows from that, not the other way around.
